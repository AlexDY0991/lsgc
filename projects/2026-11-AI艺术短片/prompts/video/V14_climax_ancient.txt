LOOK
Low-key, dark mid-tones, soft rolled-off highlights; cool grey-teal and olive, low saturation; warmth only from a window or a fire, never golden sunset light. Soft overcast base light plus one directional source, no visible light beams. Old spherical primes, shallow depth of field, out-of-focus foreground, off-centre framing. Light film grain, no sharpening, no HDR. Real textures, dust and wear. No glowing threads anywhere. No text, no logos, no signs, no subtitles.

CRAFT ACCURACY
The craft is shown exactly as it is really done, at natural working speed; every tool touches what it acts on; nothing appears, vanishes or changes shape; hands have five fingers and keep the same tool for the whole shot.
The warp is split into an upper and a lower layer; the shuttle always passes INSIDE the opening between the two layers.

SHOTS
0.0–2.0s — Macro, 100 mm, side-on to the warp at shed height: the upper and lower warp layers form a narrow opening; a boat-shaped wooden shuttle shoots through the opening from RIGHT to LEFT, thrown by a hand in a dark red sleeve that enters from the right; the other hand catches it at the left edge. Grey window light from the left. Hard cut.
2.0–4.0s — Macro, 100 mm, same side-on angle and the same size in frame as shot 1, a different loom: a hand in a grey-blue sleeve throws a darker worn shuttle through the opening from RIGHT to LEFT. Hard cut.
4.0–6.0s — Close, 65 mm, looking up: two weathered hands in undyed sleeves grip a bundle of pale cords and pull it straight UP and hold it; the cords go taut. Hard cut.
6.0–8.0s — Close, 50 mm, low at floor level: a foot in a worn cloth shoe presses a wooden treadle firmly DOWN; the treadle cord jerks; dust on the floorboards.

SOUND
No music, no voices. Shuttle slide and catch; second shuttle; creak and swish of cords; a heavy wooden thump of the treadle at 6.6s.

KEEP CONSISTENT
- Four shots, hard cuts at 2.0, 4.0, 6.0s; shuttles move right to left inside the opening; hands only, no faces; no glowing threads, no text.
