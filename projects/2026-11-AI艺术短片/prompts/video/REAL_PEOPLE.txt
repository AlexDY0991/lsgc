PEOPLE LOOK — documentary realism, not advertising
Candid moments caught by a handheld 35 mm documentary camera in available light; the people are unaware of the camera and are caught mid-gesture; focus is sometimes a little soft; framing is imperfect, heads cropped or partly hidden behind out-of-focus foreground objects. Ordinary everyday Chinese faces of different ages and body types: uneven skin, wrinkles, tired eyes, untidy hair, some wear glasses. Cheap, mismatched, worn everyday clothes: pilled sweaters, creased jackets, faded jeans, plastic slippers. Cluttered real backgrounds: plastic bags, cardboard boxes, scooters, tangled wires, stains on walls. Real motion: small hesitations, weight shifts, natural blinking, motion blur on quick hands. Never: posing, symmetrical composition, model faces, smooth or glowing skin, perfect teeth, glossy look, advertising smiles, beauty lighting, everyone looking good at once.
